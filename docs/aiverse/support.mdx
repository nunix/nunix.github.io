# The Fleet Needs You

*In the grim darkness of the far future, knowledge is the only weapon that matters.*

The AIverse chronicles are **free**. They always will be. Knowledge belongs to everyone who wants to build.

But no crusade has ever expanded on faith alone.

---

## ⚙️ The Forge Requires Fuel

Every Era in this archive was paid for in machine-time. Tokens burned by the million. Servers held at temperature through the night. Hours spent hunting a fault that turned out to be one missing `parent_id`.

The Adeptus Mechanicus do not research on goodwill. They requisition. This page is the requisition.

Your contribution buys you nothing — access is already yours, unconditionally. It buys the *next* expedition: the next system dragged into the light, the next pattern recovered from the dark, the next chronicle written down before the context window closes and takes it forever.
