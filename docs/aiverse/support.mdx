# The Fleet Needs You

*In the grim darkness of the far future, knowledge is the only weapon that matters.*

The AIverse chronicles are **free**. They always will be. Knowledge belongs to everyone who wants to build.

But fleets have costs. Servers need power. The Remembrancer needs time. If this chronicle saved you hours of architecture mistakes, or gave you a framework you actually shipped — consider contributing to the crusade.

---

## ⚔️ The Technical Companion — Gumroad

The blog tells the story. The **Technical Companion** goes deeper.

Full schemas. Complete command transcripts. Architecture Decision Records for every major choice. The uncut version of what you read here, for those who want to build exactly what we built.

**Every purchase directly funds the next chapter.**

→ **[AIverse Technical Companion on Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/nunix)**

## 🏴 Community Unlock Goal

When a funding goal is reached, the corresponding era unlocks **for everyone, permanently**. No individual paywall. Collective action, collective reward.

The idea: if enough people value the chronicle, the next chapter becomes free for the entire community. You don't just buy content — you free it.

*Watch the goal progress on the Gumroad page.*

## ☕ Direct Donations — Ko-fi or GitHub

Two bridges to the same fuel. Pick the one that fits.

**Ko-fi**: No subscription, no account needed. One click. → **[ko-fi.com/nunix](https://ko-fi.com/nunix)**

**GitHub**: Click the **Sponsor** button on the [repository](https://github.com/nunix/nunix.github.io). Routes to Ko-fi and Gumroad. Once GitHub Sponsors is enabled, direct sponsorships will flow here too — same crusade, different entry point.

All of it goes to keeping the fleet running.
